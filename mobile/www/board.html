Chaos Cities board

What is playable, what needs a real phone and friends to verify, and what we plan to add next.

✅ Playable now

Docker server, persistent cities, and Android v0.5.2 demo
320 traits, optional groups, rotating chaos choices, ten log entries per category, and unique buildings
One Chaos Token each hour; Cash earned from Wealth each hour
0.001 hourly passive growth, upgrade shop and clear lists for 51 discoveries with timed research
Daily funny city notice and highlighted best recent event reward
Common through Legendary Heroes with Guard, Rally, and Focus battle roles; one starting chair and building plot
Twenty-trait PvP with a 42-second animated reveal, funny commentary, newcomer shields, and six incoming hostile events daily
City trades for Heroes, Wealth, and goods, with ten proposals daily and four per rival; 12 skyline stages and five city specializations
Underdog fairness and trade permissions checked; AI battle commentary hides winner spoilers until the reveal
Public hosting requires a private invite code and HTTPS domain; tested private database backups
City listings and event feeds require sign-in; public sign-ups are capped per IP
Repeated PvP and trade playtest checks transfers and conservation; stolen Heroes close stale trade offers
Town Hall: weather, streaks, goals, achievements, decrees, festivals, crates, training, gifts, and rankings
Two-row phone navigation, clear research buttons, and player-facing Heroes terminology
Automatic hourly production, stored goods for buildings, research, and player trades, and expiring services
Rare alien words learned through events and people
Local Hosting homepage restored
